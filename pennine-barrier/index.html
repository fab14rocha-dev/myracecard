Route Overview

▼

Starts at Malham Showground. A looped route through the Yorkshire Dales:

  1. Malham → Malham Cove → Dry Valley of Watlowes
  2. Malham Tarn → Fountains Fell → base of Pen-y-Ghent
  3. Joins the Yorkshire 3 Peaks trail: Pen-y-Ghent → Whernside → Ingleborough
  4. Back across Fountains Fell and Malham Tarn
  5. Back to Malham Cove → turn left at the limestone pavement → Gordale
  6. Drop down via Janet's Foss → finish at Malham Showground
Route is largely unmarked. Follow GPX on your device. Download the latest GPX from the GB Ultras website before race day.

Only the first section from the showground to Malham Cove has laminated signs and biodegradable red tape.

GPS Tracker

▼

Collected at registration on race morning only. Already activated when handed to you — nothing to set up.

  • Attach it to the outside of your pack/shoulder strap, velcro provided
  • Must have line of sight to the sky — do not put it inside your bag
  • Keep it in the waterproof sealed bag — lost/damaged tracker charged at £150 + VAT
  • Return it to event team when you finish (or if you drop out)

Updates every ~2 minutes. Signal can be patchy in parts of the Dales — a flashing ! icon means no signal, not that you've gone off-course.

Family can follow your live position live from 6 AM on race day:
map.racetracker.no/?race=pennine_barrier_ultra_2026
There is an SOS button on the front of the tracker. Hold for ~3 seconds in a life-threatening emergency. This is not as effective as calling Race HQ directly.

Key Rules to Remember

▼
  • Bib number on your front at all times — visible, not folded
  • Must check in at every checkpoint (team writes your bib number + time)
  • No pacers or support runners at any point
  • No running with a dog

  • No cups provided — bring your own
  • Supporters cannot attend CP1, CP3, or CP5 — time penalty/DQ risk
  • No music through speakers (headphones OK, use common sense at road crossings)
IBUPROFEN IS PROHIBITED. Use paracetamol or other approved pain relief instead.

Emergency & Medical

▼
In a life-threatening emergency — in this order:
  1. Use thermal bag, spare clothing to stay warm
  2. Press SOS button on tracker (hold ~3 seconds)
  3. Call 999 — ask for Ambulance
  4. Call Race HQ: 07944 383383
  5. Race Directors: Laura Yates 07523 163694 / Wayne Drinkwater 07701 349501
  6. Attract attention — shout, whistle, torch

For non-emergencies: tell the marshals at the next checkpoint. They'll pass the message forward.

Mobile signal is limited in parts of the Yorkshire Dales. If you can't get through, keep moving to higher ground or toward a checkpoint.

Key things to watch: Stay hydrated with electrolytes (not just water). If urine goes dark brown, that's a medical issue — get to a checkpoint. Watch for signs of hypothermia: confusion, extreme tiredness, disorientation.

How to Track Him

▼

Fabricio carries a GPS tracker throughout the race. Follow his dot live from 6:00 AM on race day using the link below:

map.racetracker.no/?race=pennine_barrier_ultra_2026
The tracker updates every ~2 minutes and can lose signal in parts of the Yorkshire Dales. If his dot stops moving or shows a flashing !, don't panic — it just means no phone signal in that area. It'll update again once he moves into coverage.
Mobile signal is limited in some areas of the course. If you can't reach him by phone, check the tracker and trust the cutoff times in the Race Details tab.
